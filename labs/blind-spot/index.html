How this works

Camera: permission is requested only after you choose to begin. The camera feed stays in the page and is analyzed with a browser-loaded COCO-SSD object-detection model. This page does not intentionally record or upload camera frames.

What AI does: it detects visible object categories and reports confidence. After you freeze the scene, this prototype creates one deliberately limited, plausible interpretation from those labels to demonstrate how easily recognition can be mistaken for understanding. The interpretation is not presented as fact.

What it does not do: it does not identify who you are or infer sensitive personal traits such as health, race, age, emotion, personality, or identity.
