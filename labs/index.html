Inside the Labs

The future pipeline is a portfolio of decisions.

ITSBAD Labs does not publish an idea board. These are products we believe solve valuable problems and are worth advancing. We share the thesis, the market tension, and the direction. The mechanics that create the advantage stay private until they should be public.

What earns a place here

A product thesis has to survive more than excitement.

A future build advances when the problem is consequential enough, the value is credible enough, and the product can create a defensible experience—not simply demonstrate a new technology.

Problem worth solving.

There has to be meaningful friction, not just an interesting use case.

Value worth building.

The solution needs a credible path to adoption, revenue, or measurable impact.

Advantage worth protecting.

The public story explains why the product matters. The differentiating mechanics remain ours.
