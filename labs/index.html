How something leaves the lab

Evidence before enthusiasm.

Experiments graduate when they solve a clear problem, survive real use, and become simpler—not more impressive—as they mature.

Prove the need.

Start with friction people can actually feel.

Prototype the moment.

Build enough to learn where the value really is.

Earn the product.

Only then decide whether it deserves to become a product.
