Evidence behind the experience

What Flock says its LPR data can contain

Flock's current LPR policy says LPR data can include plate and vehicle images, plate number and state, vehicle characteristics such as color and make, date, time, and camera location. It says queries are audit-logged and LPR data is hard-deleted on a rolling seven-day basis by default, subject to customer law or policy. Read Flock's policy ↗

Why civil-liberties critics remain concerned

The ACLU argues that networked ALPR systems can create location-surveillance risks because repeated observations and data sharing can expose patterns of movement at scale. Read the ACLU analysis ↗

Documented alert consequence

The Plymouth sequence shown in the experience is based on a 2026 CBS Minnesota report involving an incorrect stolen-plate database entry and subsequent Flock alerts. Read the report ↗

What is simulated here

The vehicle, locations, timing, movement pattern, and interface are fictional. They illustrate the distinction between an observation, an inference drawn from observations, and proof.
