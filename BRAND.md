# ITSBAD Labs Brand Foundation v1.0

## Brand idea
Useful technology. Visible evidence. Human decisions.

## Positioning
ITSBAD Labs builds technology for things that shouldn't be this hard.

## Personality
Bold, smart, designed, useful, credible, human, and selectively playful.

## Master palette
- Ink: `#07111F`
- Signature Pink: `#FF66C4`
- Navy: `#3A3B78`
- Digital Blue: `#5FD4FF`
- Violet: `#8A7DFF`
- Paper: `#F7F8FC`

## Typography
Inter with system fallbacks. Strong typographic hierarchy, generous whitespace, uppercase tracked brand labels.

## Product architecture
ITSBAD Labs is the master brand. Products retain independent names and one product accent. Endorsement: `An ITSBAD Labs product`.

## Design principles
1. Understand it.
2. Trust it.
3. Use it.
4. Mobile is the baseline.
5. Evidence is visible.
6. No AI theater.
7. Consistency without sameness.

## Logo system
For v1, use the typography-led `ITSBAD LABS` wordmark. The compact proprietary key mark is intentionally provisional and must not block product work.
