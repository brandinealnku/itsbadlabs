About ITSBAD Labs

Technology is at its best when the complexity disappears.

ITSBAD Labs is an independent product and innovation studio focused on making difficult information, emerging technology, and fragmented systems easier for people to understand and use.

Why the Labs exist

The best products rarely come from starting with a tool. They come from understanding a frustrating problem deeply enough to remove the unnecessary complexity around it.

Built across disciplines

ITSBAD Labs brings together product strategy, visual design, software development, enterprise transformation, AI, automation, and learning design. That breadth shapes products that are both technically capable and understandable to humans.

Founded by

Dr. Brandi D. Neal

Designer, developer, enterprise technology leader, educator, and product builder.

The Labs is the place where those disciplines come together in working technology.
