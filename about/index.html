About ITSBAD Labs

Built by someone who has spent a career inside complex systems.

ITSBAD Labs is an independent product and innovation studio founded by Dr. Brandi D. Neal. The work draws on more than 15 years across enterprise technology, software, digital transformation, design, automation, AI, and higher education—then turns that experience into products that make difficult things feel obvious.

Why the Labs exist

Complexity is rarely the interesting part of a product. It is the raw material. The real work is understanding enough of the system—users, data, workflows, constraints, incentives, and technology—to decide what the experience should make disappear and what it must make visible.

Experience changes what gets built

ITSBAD Labs is shaped by work across enterprise platforms, organizational transformation, web and software development, product strategy, visual design, AI, automation, and learning systems. That range matters because important technology problems rarely stay inside one discipline.

Innovation with an operating model

The goal is not to produce impressive prototypes that stop at the demo. The goal is to move from ambiguity to a product thesis, from thesis to working system, and from working system toward something people or organizations will adopt, trust, and pay for.

Founder

Her work sits at the intersection of systems thinking, human behavior, technology strategy, and execution. ITSBAD Labs is where those disciplines are used together—without the handoff gaps that usually separate strategy from the thing that actually ships.
