OpenSlots

What if an open appointment could find demand instead of waiting to be discovered?

The problem

An appointment that opens at the last minute is a perishable asset. Once the time passes, its value disappears. Yet available time is often trapped inside individual schedules while potential customers search somewhere else.

The product thesis

OpenSlots is being built around the idea that time-sensitive availability should be more discoverable when it matters most. The product explores a marketplace relationship between open capacity and people who may be ready to act now.

Why it matters

For providers, an unfilled opening can mean lost revenue. For customers, a newly available slot can be exactly what they were hoping to find. The opportunity exists in reducing the distance between those two moments.
