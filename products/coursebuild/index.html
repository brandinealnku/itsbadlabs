CourseBuild

Build the course. Keep the judgment. CourseBuild turns the repetitive work of structuring a digital course into an assisted workflow for instructors—helping create and update Canvas pages, assignments, discussions, quizzes, modules, rubrics, and outcomes without giving up control of the course itself.

Ask about early access

From course plan to Canvas structure

CourseBuild is designed to reduce the manual setup between an instructor's course design and the learning environment students actually use.

AI where it helps. Control where it matters.

Assisted generation can accelerate repetitive work while instructor review, dry-run workflows, source material, and existing course structure keep the human decision-maker in the loop.

Built around real course operations

The product goes beyond generating copy. Current workflows include content creation and updates, course inventory, due-date and point changes, readiness checks, duplicate prevention, and recovery-oriented controls.
