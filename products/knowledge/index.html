ITSBAD Knowledge

Turn trusted information into an AI people can ask. ITSBAD Knowledge creates source-grounded assistants from approved information so organizations can make complex knowledge easier to access without pretending the AI knows more than the evidence supports.

Open current prototype

Grounded by design

Answers are generated from approved source material and designed to retain clear connections back to the evidence.

Honest when evidence stops

If approved sources do not support an answer, the system should say so rather than fill the gap with confident speculation.

From courses to organizations

The first implementation focuses on instructor-approved course materials, but the architecture can extend to other controlled knowledge environments.

Current statePrototype / productization

Core principles

  • Approved sources only
  • Citation-first answers
  • Visible build status
  • Clear knowledge boundaries
