RecallCheck

Food recalls are complicated. Checking yours shouldn't be. RecallCheck organizes recall information from trusted government sources into a clearer experience designed to help people understand whether something they bought may be affected.

Open current RecallCheck build

The problem

Recall information is authoritative, but it can be fragmented across agencies, difficult to scan, and written for regulatory precision rather than everyday decision-making.

What RecallCheck changes

It brings search, filtering, source attribution, status, and practical next steps into one understandable interface.

Built for trust

RecallCheck is being designed to distinguish record dates, source publication dates, and data retrieval checks rather than presenting freshness it cannot verify.

Current stateLive product / active refinement

Core principles

  • Trusted-source visibility
  • Mobile-first recall search
  • Clear agency and category filters
  • Calm, actionable safety language
