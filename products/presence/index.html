PIPELINE 006 · UNDER WRAPS

Presence

The web that knows you’re there.

The problem

Most websites still behave as if every meaningful interaction begins with a click, scroll, tap, or typed request. That assumption leaves a large part of human-computer interaction unexplored.

The product thesis

Presence explores a more responsive relationship between the person and the page—one where the interface can react to more than explicit input and adapt the experience in the moment.

Why it matters

As interfaces become more intelligent, the next leap may not come from adding another chatbot or button. It may come from rethinking what the page is capable of noticing, understanding, and doing.

Status: Under wraps
Built by ITSBAD Labs

Enough to understand the direction. Not enough to copy the interaction.

The product name and public thesis are designed to create a clear category signal while keeping the sensing model, interaction logic, architecture, and implementation private.
