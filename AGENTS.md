# ITSBAD Labs Repository Rules

Before changing UI:
1. Read BRAND.md and DESIGN.md.
2. Reuse existing tokens and components.
3. Design mobile-first.
4. Preserve semantic HTML, keyboard support, and visible focus.
5. Do not introduce arbitrary colors, fonts, spacing, gradients, or component languages.
6. Do not use generic AI visual motifs.
7. Do not add cards unless grouping improves comprehension.
8. Do not invent unsupported claims, customer logos, metrics, testimonials, freshness, or product status.
9. Keep product accents separate from semantic success/warning/error colors.
10. Keep proprietary logic and secrets out of client-side code.
11. Scope changes narrowly; do not redesign unrelated pages during a fix.

Before completion, test at 360px, 390px, 768px, and 1280px; test keyboard focus, overflow, long text, reduced motion, and broken/empty states where relevant.
